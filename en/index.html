The whole conversion chain, from lead to signed contract

As a B2B setter and closer, I work with web agencies, SaaS companies and service providers who generate leads but lack the time — or the method — to qualify them and turn them into clients. I also support young independents who are starting their business and need to land their first clients.

In practice, you outsource your prospecting to me: I book qualified meetings and run the closing through to signature — a single point of contact rather than an agency team.

Setter — upstream
  • Sorting and qualifying inbound and outbound leads (need, budget, decision-making power)
  • First contact, sparking interest and booking the meeting
  • Preparing the ground so the closing call starts on solid footing
Closer — downstream
  • Running closing meetings over video or phone, through to signature
  • Handling objections (price, timing, trust) without forcing the decision
  • Structuring and tracking the pipeline so no opportunity slips through

My background: 6 years managing the relationship, follow-up and tracking of hundreds of contacts in higher education, then the move into sales at MKZ (website creation, SEO, web design). I learned there that setting and closing isn't about pushing a product: it's about understanding fast, speaking plainly, and helping the prospect make the right decision — at the right point in the cycle.

I also build AI into my process (qualification, call preparation, personalising follow-ups) to gain speed and relevance.

How it works

A simple process, designed to slot into your existing pipeline without adding complexity.

  1. 1

    Scoping

    We clarify your offer, your target and the current state of your pipeline: what's working, what's stuck, where you need reinforcement.

  2. 2

    Qualification & booking

    I sort your leads (BANT / MEDDIC), open the first contact and book the meetings that have a real reason to go somewhere.

  3. 3

    Closing

    I run the calls through to signature: handling objections, supporting the decision, without forcing it.

  4. 4

    Follow-up & reporting

    You know where every opportunity stands at all times — regular reports, no black box.

How do you define a “qualified” lead?

A contact that meets two criteria: they match the target profile (industry, company size, role or decision-making power), and they show concrete interest through an engaging action — downloading a document, visiting the pricing page, requesting a demo…

What kind of clients or industries do you prefer to work with?

Primarily young independents starting their business who need to land their first clients, along with web agencies, SaaS companies and B2B service providers. The situation matters more than the industry: tradespeople, sales reps, consultants…

How long is a typical engagement?

Open to any type of engagement. So far, one-off projects only, but I'm also available for ongoing support.

How is the engagement followed up?

Active follow-up: regular progress check-ins, and a continuous line kept open between you and me until the engagement is complete.
